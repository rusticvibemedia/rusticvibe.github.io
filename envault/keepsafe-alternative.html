Honest advice

Which one is for you?

Pick Keepsafe if

  • You want a copy of your photos in the cloud.
  • You want to reset a forgotten PIN by email.
  • You also use an Android phone.

Pick Envault if

  • You do not want to pay every month.
  • You want nothing uploaded and no account at all.
  • You want notes, passwords, IDs and cards in the same private place.
  • You want a second vault that opens with a different PIN.
Moving from Keepsafe

Try it before you cancel.

Envault is free with room for 25 photos and 10 videos, so you can see how it feels first. When you are ready, bring your photos in from your gallery. Remember that Envault keeps everything on this iPhone only, with no backup, so keep your own copy of anything you could not bear to lose.
