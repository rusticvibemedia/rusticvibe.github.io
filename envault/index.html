Honest answers

Before you download.

Does Envault need the internet?

No. Everything works in Airplane Mode. The internet is involved only if you buy Envault Pro, and Apple handles that purchase.

Is there a subscription?

No. Envault is free to use. Envault Pro is a single purchase that stays yours, and you can restore it for free on your devices with your Apple Account.

What if I forget my PIN?

Then the vault cannot be opened, by you or by us. There is no recovery email and no reset. A way back in for you would also be a way in for anyone who takes your phone, so Envault does not have one. Keep your PIN somewhere safe.

What if I lose my phone?

Your things live only on that iPhone. There is no backup and no sync, so there is nothing on a server to lose or leak. It also means a lost phone is a lost vault. Keep your own copy of anything you could not bear to lose.

Can I move my vault to a new iPhone?

Not today. A vault stays on the iPhone where it was made.

Does Envault collect my data?

No. Envault has no account, no analytics and no ads. Its App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected. Read the privacy policy.
