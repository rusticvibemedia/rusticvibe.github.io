Privacy Policy

Your data never leaves your device.

Last updated: July 2026

The short version: Envault collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing about you. It has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Everything you put in the app is encrypted and stays on your device. We literally cannot see it.

Who we are

Envault is developed by RusticVibe Media LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Envault iOS app handles your information. Contact us anytime at support@rusticvibe.com.

Information we collect

None. Envault does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on any server. We have no user accounts, no sign-in, and no way to identify you. The app makes no network connections to us or to any third party for its core function.

What stays on your device

Everything you add to Envault — photos, passwords, contacts, documents, notes, and wallet records — is stored only on your device, encrypted. The encryption keys are protected by your PIN and by your device's Secure Enclave. Because this data never leaves your device and is not readable without your PIN, we have no access to it and cannot recover it.

Device permissions

Envault may ask for the following permissions. Each is used only on your device to provide a feature you chose, and never to collect or transmit data:

  • Photos — so you can import photos into a vault.
  • Camera — so you can capture a photo directly into a vault.
  • Contacts — so you can import contacts into a vault.
  • Face ID / Touch ID — as an optional second factor after your PIN. Your biometric data is handled entirely by iOS and is never seen by Envault.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

Envault contains no advertising, no third-party tracking, no analytics SDKs, and no cookies. We do not build profiles and do not share or sell anything, because we never receive anything in the first place.

Purchases

Envault Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple. Apple handles the transaction and your payment details; we never see your payment information. Apple's App Store and its privacy practices govern that purchase. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Since your data lives only on your device, you are always in control of it. Deleting an item, or deleting the Envault app, removes that data from your device. Envault's optional auto-erase feature can also wipe all vault data after a number of wrong PIN attempts. There is no cloud copy and no backup on our side to delete, because none was ever created.

Children's privacy

Envault is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here and update the date above. Because the app collects nothing, changes will typically be clarifications only.

Contact
